# Brief 2: reminders for borrowed tools

*Notes pasted in from a chat thread. The product is Mossbank, the app that community gardens use to manage plots, members and shared tools. Mossbank, the gardens and the people are made up for this exercise.*

- Gardens lend shared tools: wheelbarrows, strimmers, a rotavator at the bigger sites. Members sign a tool out in Mossbank. Many forget to sign it back in, or bring it back late.
- Coordinators have asked for automatic reminders when a tool is overdue.
- Kwame (coordinator at Hollow Lane): the rotavator was gone for "most of May" last year. That is the story everyone tells. Nobody has counted how often tools come back late.
- Loan length today is whatever the coordinator types in when the tool is signed out. It is often left blank.
- Idea: remind the borrower the day before the tool is due and the day after. If it is still out some days later, tell the coordinator.
- Kwame wants fines for late returns. Ines (product): "we are not building payments for this". Kwame has not agreed.
- Reminders would go by email. Some members say they never read email from Mossbank. The app can send push notifications, but "about half" of members only use the website (Priya's guess, not checked).
- What if the due date was left blank? Nobody said.
- Nobody said what success would look like. Ines, half joking: "fewer angry messages in the coordinators' group chat".
- Timing: "after the waiting-list work".
